# Canadian Banking Basics, PADs & Low-Balance Alerts

Navigating the Canadian retail banking system effectively helps young adults avoid costly penalty fees and build early wealth.

## First Chequing Account & No-Fee Student Accounts
- Canada's Big Five chartered banks (RBC, TD, Scotiabank, BMO, CIBC) and digital institutions (Tangerine, Simplii, EQ Bank) offer zero-fee student or youth chequing accounts with unlimited Interac e-Transfers and debit transactions.
- Always provide updated proof of student enrollment annually to maintain no-fee student status and avoid standard monthly account maintenance fees ($10.95–$16.95/mo).

## Pre-Authorized Debits (PADs) and the $10 NSF Cap
A Pre-Authorized Debit (PAD) is an arrangement authorizing a biller (such as your landlord, phone carrier, auto insurer, or gym) to withdraw money directly from your chequing account on a scheduled date.
- **2026 Federal NSF Fee Protection:** Under Canadian federal banking regulations, chartered banks have capped Non-Sufficient Funds (NSF) overdraft refusal fees at **$10.00** (down from historical $45–$48 fees), and banks must provide mandatory low-balance alerts when your account dips below $100.
- **The Biller Late Fee Trap:** Even with the $10 bank NSF cap, your biller (e.g., Rogers, Bell, Enbridge, or your landlord) will charge their own returned payment fee ($25 to $50) plus late interest, and may report a missed payment to Equifax or TransUnion.
- **Nyra’s PAD Strategy:** Nyra 2.0 flags PAD bills 3 to 5 days before withdrawal so you can check your chequing balance and transfer funds before the debit hits.

## Tax-Free Savings Accounts (TFSA) in Canada
- Every Canadian resident aged 18 or older with a valid Social Insurance Number automatically accumulates annual TFSA contribution room.
- The **2026 annual TFSA contribution room is $7,000**.
- All capital gains, dividends, and interest earned inside a TFSA are **100% tax-free** upon withdrawal, making it the premier starter investment and emergency fund vehicle for Canadian young adults.
