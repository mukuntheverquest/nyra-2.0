# Financial Futures Education (FFE) Mission & The 20% Impact Pool

Financial Futures Education (FFE) is an Ontario-based social enterprise co-founded by Sanya Bajaj and Aryan Kapur, dedicated to breaking generational cycles of financial vulnerability among underserved youth.

## The Problem FFE Solves
Standard high school curricula rarely prepare young adults for real-world Canadian personal finance. Underserved youth — especially those aging out of the foster care system with Children’s Aid Societies (CAS), youth transitioning out of provincial detention centres, and young newcomers — often face the adult financial system without guidance, leaving them vulnerable to predatory lending, debt traps, and cyber fraud.

## FFE's Educational Model
FFE delivers hands-on, trauma-informed, customized financial literacy workshops directly to partner organizations across Ontario and Canada.
- **Customized Cohorts:** Interactive sessions covering budgeting for independent living, Ontario payroll & tax filing, credit building without predatory traps, and navigating post-secondary funding.
- **Institutional Partnerships:** Universities (e.g., McMaster University), student unions, student affairs offices, and community social service agencies.
- **Proven Curriculum:** Tested directly with youth transitioning to independence to maximize practical retention and confidence.

## Nyra 2.0 & The 20% Net-Profit Youth Impact Pool
Nyra 2.0 was built as both a daily financial safety harness for young adults and a sustainable funding engine for FFE's grassroots mission.
- **20% Mission Commitment:** Exactly **20% of net profits** generated from Nyra 2.0 subscriptions (organic consumer plans at $5/month or $45/year, plus B2B2C institutional campus seats at ~$10/seat/year) are directed directly into FFE's community impact fund.
- **Real-World Impact:** Every subscriber directly funds free financial literacy workshops for youth who cannot afford them.
  - A single $1,500 workshop equips 20–30 at-risk youth with essential financial lifeskills.
  - Full semester cohort partnerships ($7,000+) provide longitudinal coaching and mentorship.
- **The Nyra Streak:** Every time a user confirms an on-time bill payment or sets a trial kill-switch in Nyra 2.0, their payment streak increments and adds funded workshop minutes to the community counter.
