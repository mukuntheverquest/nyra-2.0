# Canadian Credit Scores & Student Credit Cards

Your credit score is a three-digit reputation score reflecting how reliably you handle borrowed money and ongoing service contracts in Canada.

## Credit Score Ranges in Canada
Canada’s two primary credit reporting agencies are **Equifax Canada** and **TransUnion Canada**. Scores range from **300 to 900**:
- **750 – 900 (Excellent):** Preferred rates on auto loans, mortgages, and premium card approvals.
- **660 – 749 (Good):** Qualifies smoothly for student apartment leases, phone plans, and standard credit products.
- **560 – 659 (Fair):** May encounter security deposit demands from telecom carriers or require a guarantor/co-signer for apartment leases.
- **300 – 559 (Poor):** High risk of rejection for rental leases, credit cards, and post-paid mobile contracts.

## Statement Date vs. Due Date
- **Statement Date (Closing Date):** The date your monthly billing cycle closes. The balance on this date is reported to Equifax and TransUnion.
- **Due Date:** The deadline (usually 21 days after the statement date, known as the grace period) by which you must pay at least the minimum payment or full statement balance.
- **The Golden Rule:** Always pay the **full statement balance** on or before the due date to avoid standard credit card purchase interest of **19.99% to 22.99% APR** (or 27.99% on cash advances).

## The 30% Credit Utilization Benchmark
Credit utilization is the percentage of your total available credit limit you use.
- If your student credit card limit is $1,000, keeping your reported statement balance under **$300 (30%)** optimizes your credit score growth.
- Utilizing 90%+ of your card limit signals financial distress to credit algorithms, suppressing your credit score even if you pay in full every month.

## The 6-Year Credit Impact of Missed Bills
- Missed payments on credit cards, student loans, and post-paid mobile carrier bills (Rogers, Bell, Telus, Fido, Freedom) remain on your Canadian credit bureau history for **up to 6 years**.
- Collection agency records and delinquencies severely damage your ability to rent apartments, get vehicle financing, or pass employment credit checks in finance and government sectors.
